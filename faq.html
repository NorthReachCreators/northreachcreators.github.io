Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What platforms do you work with?

We specialize in YouTube and Instagram, as these platforms drive the highest engagement for AI/Tech brands. Our creator network focuses primarily on these two platforms where tech-savvy audiences spend most of their time.

What types of creators are in your network?

Our network consists exclusively of male AI/Tech content creators with audiences ranging from 25k to 4M+ followers. They create educational content, tutorials, product reviews, and tech news commentary. All creators produce high-quality, authentic content that resonates with engaged tech audiences.

How long does it take to launch a campaign?

From initial contact to content going live typically takes 2-4 weeks. This includes strategy development, creator matching, contract negotiation, content creation, approval rounds, and publishing. Timelines vary based on campaign complexity and creator availability.

What is your commission structure?

We operate on a transparent 20-30% commission model. This covers full campaign management including creator vetting, contract negotiation, content coordination, performance tracking, and payment processing. Final rates depend on campaign scope and deliverables.

Do you offer customer support?

Yes! Every brand and creator gets dedicated support throughout the entire campaign. We're available via email and respond within 24 hours. For active campaigns, we provide regular updates and are available for urgent questions or adjustments.

Why don't you work with female influencers?

NorthReach Creators operates according to halal business principles. As part of our faith-based practices, we work exclusively with male creators and do not include music in paid integrations. We're transparent about this upfront to ensure alignment with all partners.

What is the minimum campaign budget?

Our minimum campaign budget is $1,000 USD. This ensures we can match you with quality creators who have proven track records and engaged audiences. Most successful campaigns range from $5,000-$25,000 depending on creator reach and deliverables.

Can I choose specific creators?

Absolutely! We present you with 2-4 hand-selected creators who match your brand values and target audience. You review their channels, engagement metrics, and past work before making a final decision. We never commit you to a creator without your approval.
