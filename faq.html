Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What platforms do you work with?

We work across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, and newsletters — wherever tech-savvy audiences actually spend their time and trust what they're reading or watching.

What types of creators are in your network?

Our network is 30+ AI/tech content creators with a combined reach of 15M+, producing educational content, tutorials, product reviews, and tech commentary that resonates with engaged, tech-savvy audiences.

How long does it take to launch a campaign?

From initial contact to content going live typically takes 2–4 weeks, covering strategy, creator matching, contract negotiation, content creation, approval, and publishing. Timelines vary with campaign complexity and creator availability.

What is your pricing model?

We operate on a transparent flat-fee markup — not commission. This covers full campaign management including creator vetting, contract negotiation, content coordination, and performance tracking. Pricing is discussed upfront before any commitment.

Do you offer customer support?

Yes. Every brand and creator gets dedicated support throughout the campaign. We're reachable by email and respond within 24 hours, with regular updates for active campaigns.

Why is your creator network male-only?

NorthReachCreators is a halal-first agency: no adult content, no alcohol, and no gambling in any partnership, and our creator network is exclusively male. We're upfront about this so brands and creators know what to expect before working with us.

Can I choose specific creators?

Absolutely. We present a shortlist of hand-selected creators who match your brand values and target audience. You review their channels and past work before making a final decision — we never commit you to a creator without your approval.

Still Have Questions?

We're here to help. Reach out and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.
