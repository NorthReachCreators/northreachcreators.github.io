Our Story

Building bridges between innovative AI/tech brands and the creators who understand them best.

How We Started

NorthReachCreators was founded in April 2026 by Mohammad in Cambridge, Ontario, with a vision to change how AI and tech brands connect with their audiences.

Having watched the explosive growth of AI tools alongside a disconnect between innovative products and the creators who could authentically showcase them, Mohammad saw an opportunity. Most influencer agencies treat tech sponsorships like any other campaign — but AI/tech content needs creators who understand the space, and audiences who trust their expertise.

What started as a passion project has grown into a full-service agency. We're building a reputation for campaigns that feel authentic, not forced — with transparency, integrity, and halal principles at our core.

Our Mission

We exist to simplify influencer marketing for AI/tech brands. No messy contracts, no creator vetting headaches, no campaigns that miss the mark.

We handle everything — from matching you with the right creators to negotiating contracts, managing deliverables, and tracking performance. You focus on building great products. We'll make sure the right people hear about them.

Our Values

Trust

We operate with complete transparency — no hidden fees, no surprises, just honest partnerships built on mutual respect.

Quality

Every creator in our network is hand-selected for authenticity, engagement, and expertise in the AI/tech space.

Halal-First Integrity

No adult content, no alcohol, no gambling, and a creator network that's exclusively male — ethical standards we don't compromise on.

Results

We're not here to run vanity campaigns. Every partnership is designed to drive real engagement, conversions, and ROI.

Let's Build Something Together

Whether you're a brand looking to grow or a creator ready for your next partnership, we'd love to hear from you.
