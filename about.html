Our Story

Building bridges between innovative AI/Tech brands and the creators who understand them best.

How We Started

NorthReachCreators was founded in April 2026 by Mohammad, a 19-year-old entrepreneur with a vision to revolutionize how AI and tech brands connect with their audiences.

Having witnessed the explosive growth of AI tools and the disconnect between innovative products and the creators who could authentically showcase them, Mohammad saw an opportunity. He recognized that most influencer agencies treated tech sponsorships like any other campaign—but AI/Tech content requires creators who truly understand the space and audiences who trust their expertise.

What started as a passion project quickly evolved into a full-service agency. Today, we're building a reputation for delivering results-driven campaigns that feel authentic, not forced. We're still growing, still learning, and still committed to doing business the right way—with transparency, integrity, and halal principles at our core.

Our Mission

We exist to simplify influencer marketing for AI/Tech brands. No messy contracts. No creator vetting headaches. No campaigns that miss the mark.

We handle everything—from matching you with the perfect creators to negotiating contracts, managing deliverables, and tracking performance. You focus on building great products. We'll make sure the right people hear about them.
